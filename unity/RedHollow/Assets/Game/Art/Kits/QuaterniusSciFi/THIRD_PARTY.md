Original kit albedo is replaced at instance time with Red Hollow URP Lit materials using authored Lykos maps (`hab-block-wall`, `hab-block-cladding`, `hab-block-roof`, `colony-decking`, `metal-floor-plate`, `colony-wall`, `cavern-ground`).
